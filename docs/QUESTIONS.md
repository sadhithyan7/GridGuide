# 10 real questions my user will ask

Written the way a shift engineer at a State Load Despatch Centre would type them.
At least 3 need more than one page to answer; Q10 cannot be answered from CERC documents.

| # | Question | Where the answer is (document, page) | Needs more than one page? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the permitted system frequency band under the current Indian Electricity Grid Code, and what actions must the RLDC take if frequency falls below the lower limit? | IEGC (Clause 5.2, 5.3) | Yes – frequency band table + RLDC duty clauses span multiple sections |
| 2 | How are deviation charges calculated when a generating station over-injects beyond its schedule in time-block 3 of the day? | DSM Regulations 2022 and amendments (Regulation 6, Schedule I) | Yes – formula + frequency-slab table both needed |
| 3 | ग्रिड कोड के अनुसार, शेड्यूल में संशोधन (revision) कितने समय पहले किया जा सकता है और कितनी बार? | IEGC (Clause 5.5 – Scheduling and Despatch) | No |
| 4 | What is the minimum corridor capacity that must be kept available for real-time market (RTM) transactions on an inter-state transmission corridor? | CERC Power Market Regulations 2021, RTM provisions (Regulation 10) | No |
| 5 | Under the General Network Access (GNA) Regulations, what is the procedure for a generating company to apply for long-term GNA, and what documents must be submitted? | GNA Regulations 2022 (Regulation 5–7) | Yes – application checklist, fees, and timelines across several regulations |
| 6 | डेविएशन सेटलमेंट मैकेनिज़्म के तहत, यदि कोई बिजली वितरण कंपनी (DISCOM) अपने शेड्यूल से अधिक बिजली खींचती है और उस समय ग्रिड की फ्रीक्वेंसी 49.85 Hz से कम है, तो उस पर क्या शुल्क लगेगा? | DSM Regulations 2022, Schedule I (Deviation Charge Slabs for buyers) | No |
| 7 | What are the obligations of a State Load Despatch Centre (SLDC) during a grid disturbance, and within what time must it report the incident to the RLDC? | IEGC (Clause 6 – Grid Disturbance) | No |
| 8 | Can a captive generating plant (CGP) participate in the Day-Ahead Market (DAM) on the power exchange, and if so, what open-access approvals are required? | CERC Open Access Regulations + Power Market Regulations (cross-reference) | Yes – open-access eligibility + power-exchange membership rules across two regulations |
| 9 | What is the maximum permissible deviation (in percentage of schedule) allowed for a solar generator under the current DSM framework before charges apply? | DSM Regulations 2022, 1st Amendment 2025, Schedule I (RE generator slabs) | No |
| 10 | What is the real-time electricity spot price (price per unit) on the IEX exchange right now for the current time-block? | Cannot be answered — CERC regulations do not contain live market prices; this requires a live exchange data feed | None |
